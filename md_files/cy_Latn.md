| --- | --- |
| **Cysyniadu** | **Conceptualization** |
| Syniadau; llunio neu esblygu nodau ymchwil hollgyffredinol. | Ideas; formulation or evolution of overarching research goals and aims. |
| **Curadu Data** | **Data Curation** |
| Gweithgareddau rheoli i anodi (cynhyrchu metadata), glanhau data a chynnal data ymchwil (gan gynnwys cod meddalwedd, lle mae'n angenrheidiol ar gyfer dehongli'r data ei hun) i'w ddefnyddio'n gychwynnol ac i'w ailddefnyddio'n ddiweddarach. | Management activities to annotate (produce metadata), scrub data and maintain research data (including software code, where it is necessary for interpreting the data itself) for initial use and later re-use. |
| **Dadansoddiad Ffurfiol** | **Formal Analysis** |
| Cymhwyso technegau ystadegol, mathemategol, cyfrifiadurol, neu dechnegau ffurfiol eraill i ddadansoddi neu syntheseiddio data o astudiaeth. | Application of statistical, mathematical, computational, or other formal techniques to analyse or synthesize study data. |
| **Caffael Cyllid** | **Funding Acquisition** |
| Caffael y cymorth ariannol ar gyfer y prosiect sy'n arwain at y cyhoeddiad yma. | Acquisition of the financial support for the project leading to this publication. |
| **Ymchwiliad** | **Investigation** |
| Cynnal proses ymchwil ac ymchwilio, perfformio'r arbrofion yn benodol, neu gasglu data/tystiolaeth. | Conducting a research and investigation process, specifically performing the experiments, or data/evidence collection. |
| **Methodoleg** | **Methodology** |
| Datblygu neu ddylunio methodoleg; creu modelau. | Development or design of methodology; creation of models. |
| **Gweinyddu Prosiect** | **Project Administration** |
| Cyfrifoldeb rheoli a chydlynu am gynllunio a gweithredu'r gweithgareddau ymchwil. | Management and coordination responsibility for the research activity planning and execution. |
| **Adnoddau** | **Resources** |
| Darparu deunyddiau astudio, adweithyddion, deunyddiau, cleifion, samplau labordy, anifeiliaid, offeryniaeth, adnoddau cyfrifiadurol, neu offer dadansoddi eraill. | Provision of study materials, reagents, materials, patients, laboratory samples, animals, instrumentation, computing resources, or other analysis tools. |
| **Meddalwedd** | **Software** |
| Rhaglennu, datblygu meddalwedd; dylunio rhaglenni cyfrifiadurol; gweithredu'r cod cyfrifiadurol ac algorithmau ategol; profi cydrannau cod presennol. | Programming, software development; designing computer programs; implementation of the computer code and supporting algorithms; testing of existing code components. |
| **Goruchwyliaeth** | **Supervision** |
| Cyfrifoldeb goruchwylio ac arweinyddiaeth ar gyfer cynllunio a gweithredu'r gweithgaredd ymchwil, gan gynnwys mentora y tu allan i'r tîm craidd. | Oversight and leadership responsibility for the research activity planning and execution, including mentorship external to the core team. |
| **Dilysiad** | **Validation** |
| Gwirio, boed fel rhan o'r gweithgaredd neu ar wahân, o ddyblygu/atgynhyrchadwyedd cyffredinol canlyniadau/arbrofion ac allbynnau ymchwil eraill. | Verification, whether as a part of the activity or separate, of the overall replication/reproducibility of results/experiments and other research outputs. |
| **Delweddu** | **Visualization** |
| Paratoi, creu a/neu gyflwyno'r gwaith cyhoeddedig, yn benodol delweddu/cyflwyno data. | Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work, specifically visualization/data presentation. |
| **Ysgrifennu – Draft Gwreiddiol** | **Writing – Original Draft Preparation** |
| Paratoi, creu a/neu gyflwyno'r gwaith cyhoeddedig, yn benodol ysgrifennu'r drafft cychwynnol (gan gynnwys cyfieithiad sylweddol). | Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work, specifically writing the initial draft (including substantive translation). |
| **Ysgrifennu – Adolygu & Golygu** | **Writing – Review & Editing** |
| Paratoi, creu a/neu gyflwyno'r gwaith cyhoeddedig gan y rhai o'r grŵp ymchwil gwreiddiol, yn benodol adolygiad, sylwebaeth neu adolygiad beirniadol – gan gynnwys camau cyn neu ar ôl cyhoeddi. | Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work by those from the original research group, specifically critical review, commentary or revision – including pre- or post-publication stages. |
